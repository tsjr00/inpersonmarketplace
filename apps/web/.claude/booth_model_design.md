## 1. Business rules (log in decisions.md; every rule gets a test pin)
- **BR-1 Manager veto, once.** At ANY managed market a vendor needs an APPROVED roster row before (a) activating declaration days there and (b) booking a week or season there. One-time; never per rental. *Reverses* 2026-09-05 (free managed markets zero-friction) and the 2026-09-18 middle path — owner 2026-09-19: "change authorized… we need market managers on our side." Free and fee-charging managed markets are treated the same. Off-app markets (no manager): unchanged, no gate. Events: unchanged (no manager).
- **BR-2 Application content.** Apply records: market agreement (+ platform clauses), document-sharing opt-in (optional, built 09-18), and — at FM markets with priced tiers — a **requested booth size** (tier). Purchase intent is no longer an application path: a booking attempt before approval is refused with the Apply door (BR-1).
- **BR-3 Approval content.** Approving sets `approved=true`, the vendor's tier (requested, or the size the manager can offer), a booth number (a PIN, BR-5) and an optional note to the vendor. Manager may approve with a different size than requested; the note says why. Deny = today's non-approval (row stays pending/revoked). No "awaiting vendor confirmation" state: **booking is the vendor's acceptance** of the size and number shown to them; disagreement → message the manager.
- **BR-4 Tier lock.** If the vendor's pin carries a tier, the one-off and season booking routes accept ONLY that tier (400 with the assigned size named) and the booking page pre-selects and locks it. Pin without tier → vendor chooses (today).
- **BR-5 Pin vs assignment; payment writes the pin.** A pin is a soft hold. A PAID booking makes its number the vendor's assignment: on the paid flip, if the vendor has no pin → set pin = rental number (+ tier = rental tier); if the vendor's pin differs from the rental number (yield case, BR-6) → transfer.
- **BR-6 Capacity + yielding.** Capacity per tier/week = placeholders in tier + active bookings in tier (mig 186 `:99-116`, unchanged). Pins never count. Auto-assign: (1) own pin if any (unchanged); (2) else smallest label not used this week by an active booking, a placeholder, or ANY pin; (3) else smallest label held by a SOFT pin (holder has no paid booking that week AND no paid current/upcoming week under it) — the booking takes that label for the week. On that booking's PAYMENT the pin transfers (old holder's `booth_number`/`inventory_id` cleared; new renter's set) and old vendor, new vendor and manager are notified. If the yielding booking is never paid, nothing transfers; the old pin stands. Season lock-in = paid weeks → never soft → never yields.
- **BR-7 Assignments are frozen.** While a vendor holds an assignment, the manager cannot change that vendor's pin (`vendor-booth`) and cannot override the number on any of that vendor's paid current/upcoming weeks (`weekly-rental`). Refusal copy names the rule and the way out: wait for a missed week, or cancel the paid week (BR-10). Pending (unpaid) weeks may still be overridden.
- **BR-8 Every booth-number change notifies the vendor** (in_app + email): pin set at approval; assignment on payment; transfer (old holder, new holder, manager); pin dropped by manager; per-week override; revoke. Each notification says WHAT changed, WHY (which rule) and WHAT TO DO. Manager gets a copy on transfers and on payments that create an assignment. Free channels only (in_app + email) per the 2026-07-17 comms rule; no SMS.
- **BR-9 FM cancelled market day → automatic credit for paid one-off weeks** (mirrors FT mig 201). For each PAID one-off booking (`group_id IS NULL`) whose week covers the date AND whose vendor's declaration covers that weekday: credit = round(vendor-paid amount for the week ÷ number of operating days in that week the vendor DECLARED) — never for an undeclared day, never for a past day, total per booking capped at what the vendor paid (§6-3) — granted at cancel time at THAT market, `source='fm_date_cancel'`, `related_rental_id` = the booking, one grant per (booking, date) enforced by a partial unique index, `expires_at` NULL (manager-caused; mirrors FT). Vendor notified with the amount. Season/partial bookings (`group_id` set): the cap-based settlement stands (2026-06-12 / 06-27) — the cancelled-day counter keeps feeding it; no per-day credit.
- **BR-10 Paid one-off week — vendor bears the risk (C11 = B).** No vendor cancel path; no refund; the booked week is theirs to use or lose. The MANAGER may cancel a paid one-off week: booking → 'cancelled' (slot freed that week; counts as a missed week), credit = the vendor-paid amount for the REMAINING declared days of that week (past days already attended are not credited; a cancel before the week starts = the FULL amount the vendor paid), computed with the §6-3 rule; any credit already applied to the booking is released too; total never exceeds what they paid; `source='manager_week_cancel'`, `related_rental_id`, no expiry, vendor notified with amount + manager's reason. Manager copy warns the credit comes out of their future receipts. Season groups: unchanged (vendor self-cancel route + cap settlement).
- **BR-11 Same vendor never conflicts with themself.** Trigger arms (a)/(c) and `checkBoothNumberAvailable` exclude rows of the same `vendor_profile_id`. A pinned vendor books their own booth (C1); a manager pins a vendor to the booth that vendor already rents (C7).
- **BR-13 Declared days before booking.** At a managed market, a vendor must have at least one active declaration row (`vendor_market_schedules`) for that market before booking a week or season there (owner 2026-09-19, "C"). Order of the gates in both booking routes: Stripe-ready → approved roster (BR-1) → declared days (BR-13) → tier lock (BR-4) → capacity. Keeps the credit math (§6-3) well-defined and closes the "paid but can't sell" gap fully.
- **BR-12 Revoke clears the pin** (`booth_number`, `inventory_id` → NULL) and notifies the vendor (BR-8). Revoke is refused while the vendor holds an assignment? **No** — revoke is the manager's veto and must always work; it cancels nothing paid. Paid weeks keep their number for their weeks (the vendor still has the right they paid for, BR-10 stands), but the vendor loses the pin going forward.

## 7. FT fix — spot-booking respects an active standing hold (F1) · designed 2026-09-19 · NOT built · may ride with part A or be backlogged
**The defect (cited):** `api/vendor/markets/[id]/book-park-spot/route.ts` never reads `park_standing_reservations` (0 references). A hold protects its spot only through the pending occurrence the daily sweep creates, and the sweep creates it only within `PARK_STANDING_GENERATION_HORIZON_DAYS = 7` (`park-standing.ts:31, :351`). A one-off truck can therefore book the anchor's spot for that weekday 8+ days out; when the sweep reaches that date it finds the slot taken and `continue`s (`:375-379`) — no notification to anyone. The anchor silently loses the week; the operator never learns.
**Why we are changing it (defensible statement):** a standing hold is a manager-APPROVED promise of "this spot, this weekday" (mig 173). The platform must not hand that spot to someone else before the anchor has had the chance to pay or forfeit it. This is the FT twin of FM's pin rule (BR-6: auto-assign skips pinned numbers for every future week), applied to a per-date spot model.
**Rule F1-1 (block):** a one-off booking of spot S on date D is refused when an `active` standing hold exists for (S, weekday of D) held by ANOTHER vendor, D ≥ the hold's `requested_start_date` (NULL = no floor), and the anchor has not forfeited D — forfeited = an occurrence row exists for that hold on D with status `expired` or `cancelled`. Same vendor as the hold → allowed (they are booking their own spot early; the sweep's "already have a booking for this hold on this date" check `:372-378` then skips generation — verify it matches on `standing_reservation_id`, so an early one-off by the anchor will NOT be matched; add: skip when the anchor holds ANY active booking on (S, D)). Holds in `requested` status (not yet approved) do not block. 409 `ERR_PARK_SPOT_HELD`: "Spot A is held by a recurring truck on Saturdays. It opens to other trucks only if they don't pay by the Thursday before — check back after {cutoff date}, or pick another spot."
**Rule F1-2 (notify on skip — belt and suspenders):** in the sweep, when the slot is already occupied at generation time (`:379`), notify the anchor (`park_standing_occurrence_skipped`: "Your Saturday {date} at Spot A couldn't be reserved — another truck already holds that spot that day. The operator can move you.") and the operator (`park_standing_occurrence_skipped_manager`). Covers bookings made before this fix deploys and any race at the horizon boundary. in_app + email.
**Rule F1-3 (page):** `book-spot/page.tsx` marks spots with active holds ("Held on Saturdays — recurring truck") so trucks aren't invited to pick a date that will be refused; disabled dates per held weekday in the picker. Server remains authoritative.
**Surfaces:** route check (one query per booking on `park_standing_reservations` + one on `park_spot_bookings` for forfeits), sweep notification, page badge, two notification types. **No migration.** Test pin: `book-park-spot/route.ts` references `park_standing_reservations`; `park-standing.ts` skip path calls `sendNotification`. Registry row + printable-list block: "one-off truck cannot book a held spot on its weekday beyond 7 days; anchor is told when their week is skipped."
**Placement:** independent of the FM booth work (different tables, different route). Recommended: ride in the same push as part A as its own commit so the two rounds don't drift; if deferred, this section + `backlog.md` entry are the record.
