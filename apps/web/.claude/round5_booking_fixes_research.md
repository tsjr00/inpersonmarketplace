## 2. What the thorough read changed or confirmed
- TR-156 confirmed: the page has `returnRentalId` (`:95`) but never loads the rental; the form's success block has no booth prop. The notification side already solved this in May (`types.ts:985-991` + `webhooks.ts:1716`). The booth number exists on the row from booking time (RPC, `route.ts:347-360`; bookings page prints it).
- TR-157 confirmed: `pin` carries no paid fact; the only paid-week truth helper is `getPaidWeekPairs` (used by buyer visibility). The roster's paid/unpaid split (`VendorBoothList`) is the wording to mirror.
- TR-158 confirmed + one design constraint found: the cancel flash REPLACES the form (`:214-277`) and its exits are Continue payment / Back to dashboard (or "Back to booking" → `/book`). An on-load version of that same full-page box would loop (every visit to `/book` shows it) and would stop the vendor from booking a DIFFERENT week while one is pending. So the on-load notice must sit INSIDE the form, above the week picker, with the pending week pre-selected — not reuse the full-page cancel state.
- Orphan rows (no session id) are deleted by the route on Stripe failure (`:576-636`) or swept at 30 min; the resume route refuses them (`pending-booth-rental.ts:52`). The on-load notice must therefore only surface pending rows WITH a session id.
- Nothing money-side changes: no route, no RPC, no webhook, no Stripe call. Two non-protected files.
